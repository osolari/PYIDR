\abstract{
We propose a fully Bayesian, hierarchical nonparametric extension of the Irreproducible Discovery Rate (IDR) framework of \citet{LiBrownHuangBickel2011} that simultaneously accommodates an arbitrary number of replicates $K \geq 2$, heterogeneous and possibly non-Gaussian marginals across replicates, and an unknown number of distinct dependence regimes within the reproducible component. The reproducible-component copula is modeled as a Pitman--Yor mixture of Gaussian copulas with structured correlation, augmented with $t_5$, Clayton, and Gumbel atoms for tail flexibility. Per-replicate marginals are modeled by independent Bernstein--Dirichlet priors, with degree selected by a hierarchical prior. Inference proceeds by slice-sampled P\'olya-urn MCMC (Neal Algorithm 8) for cluster reassignment, combined with NUTS on the unconstrained Cholesky parameterization of within-cluster atomic correlation matrices; a structured mean-field variational alternative with stochastic natural gradients is provided for genomic-scale data. We establish (i) generic identifiability up to label permutation under a positive-lower-orthant-dependence constraint, via Sklar's theorem coupled with the Yakowitz--Spragins linear-independence criterion; (ii) Hellinger posterior contraction at rate $\epsilon_n = n^{-\beta/(2\beta+K)}(\log n)^t$ following \citet{ShenTokdarGhosal2013} and the Pitman--Yor adaptation of \citet{Scricciolo2014BAPY}; (iii) asymptotic Bayes-mFDR control of a Sun--Cai-type plug-in step-up rule, where the rate-to-mFDR bridge is supplied by \citet{HellerRosset2021} and \citet{CastilloRoquain2020}; and (iv) Harris ergodicity of the slice-sampled sampler, with geometric ergodicity stated as a conjecture in the spirit of recent quantitative-mixing results for nonparametric mixture samplers. We design an empirical evaluation spanning the S1--S4 benchmark of \citet{LiBrownHuangBickel2011} extended with a heavy-tailed scenario S5 at $K \in \{2, 5, 10, 50\}$, together with five real datasets (ENCODE 4 ChIP-seq for CTCF, ENCODE 4 ATAC-seq, the DREAM5 challenge, Tabula Muris pseudobulk, and Pan-UK Biobank ancestry-stratified GWAS). The present paper develops the model, inference scheme, and theory, and lays out the empirical protocol with illustrative figures and placeholder result tables; final numerical values will be reported in a companion experimental paper. Code implementing the sampler will be released under an open-source license.
}
