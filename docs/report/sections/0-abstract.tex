\abstract{
We propose PY-IDR, a hierarchical Bayesian nonparametric extension of the
Irreproducible Discovery Rate (IDR) framework of
\citet{LiBrownHuangBickel2011}. PY-IDR is designed for an arbitrary number
of replicates $K \geq 2$, replicate-specific and non-Gaussian marginal
score distributions, and an unknown number of reproducible-component
dependence regimes. The reproducible copula is modelled as a
Pitman--Yor mixture of Gaussian copulas with structured correlation
matrices, augmented with $t_5$, Clayton, and Gumbel atoms for
tail-dependence robustness; replicate marginals are modelled by
Bernstein--Dirichlet priors with a hierarchical degree prior. Inference
proceeds through a Pitman--Yor P\'olya-urn MCMC scheme with auxiliary
atoms, NUTS or Metropolis updates on valid correlation parameterisations,
and a finite-truncation structured variational alternative for large
genomic studies. The theory establishes finite-sieve identifiability
under positive lower-orthant dependence and explicit
component-separation assumptions, gives a conditional Hellinger
contraction statement under stated KL-support and sieve-tail
conditions, connects posterior local-idr consistency to Bayes-mFDR
control under threshold regularity assumptions, and separates Harris
ergodicity from a conjectural geometric-rate strengthening. The
empirical contribution is two-fold. First, a \emph{submission-scale}
Monte Carlo evaluation covering the S1--S5 simulation regimes plus the
S5-sparse stress test reports realized FDR, power, and Hellinger
contraction for PY-IDR (MCMC) against four directly-integrated
comparators --- Vanilla IDR \citep{LiBrownHuangBickel2011}, MaxRank in
the spirit of \citet{Philtron2018MaRR}, ChIP-R in the spirit of
\citet{Newell2021ChIPR}, and the enhanced coefficient of variation
(eCV) of \citet{Iakovidis2024eCV} --- together with a label-switching
diagnostic for the multi-cluster posterior in the spirit of
\citet{Stephens2000Relabel}. The reproducible pipeline that produces
every figure and table is shipped with the code release alongside a
verdict ledger that records the pass / warn / fail outcome of each
pre-registered hypothesis against the recorded numerical results. One
substantive empirical finding from the submission-scale slice survives
scale-up and is reported here: among the integrated comparators, only
PY-IDR, MaxRank, and Vanilla IDR produce raw scores that are
Sun--Cai-compatible at the nominal level; eCV and ChIP-R require the
separate FDR-control layers their upstream packages ship (IDR-EM and a
rank-product null + BH respectively), and feeding their raw scores
directly into the Sun--Cai step-up rule yields realised FDR of $0.22$
to $0.75$ across regimes. Second, the
manuscript specifies the protocol for five real-data applications
(ENCODE~4 CTCF ChIP-seq, ENCODE~4 ATAC-seq, DREAM5, Tabula~Muris
pseudobulk, and Pan-UK~Biobank GWAS) and reports them as
planned-protocol work pending the upstream data manifests in
plan~08 of the implementation roadmap. The remaining cells in the
$K\in\{2,5,10,50\}\times n\in\{2{,}000,10{,}000,50{,}000\}$ factorial
design are queued for the production-scale GPU run.
}
