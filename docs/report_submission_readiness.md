## What "submission-ready" means here

This is a **methods + protocol** manuscript with a **submission-scale
methodology-verification** simulation slice. The slice exercises the
full pipeline (simulation generators, comparator integrations, sweep
driver, verdict ledger, reproducibility outputs) end-to-end on real
artefacts. The headline empirical claims of the paper (heavy-tail
dominance, calibration at production scale) are explicitly deferred
to a companion experimental paper after W10's chain-loop refactor
lands. The narrative is honest about this distinction throughout.

## How to compile

`docs/report/main.tex` is a self-contained LaTeX project. From the
submission machine:

```
cd docs/report
latexmk -pdf -interaction=nonstopmode main.tex
```

All `\input` directives, `\cite{*}` keys, and `\ref{*}` labels are
verified to resolve.
